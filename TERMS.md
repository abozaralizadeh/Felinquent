## 1. What Felinquent is

A desktop pet that does whatever it wants. It walks on your windows, sits on what you're reading and gets in your way on purpose. It never types, clicks, scrolls, moves your cursor, closes or resizes other apps, or takes screenshots. The rules it follows are in [docs/safety-and-privacy.md](docs/safety-and-privacy.md), and the panic hotkey or **Quit Felinquent** in the menu stops it at once.
